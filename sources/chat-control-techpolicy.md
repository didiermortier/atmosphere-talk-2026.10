Analysis of Chat Control's cross-border implications, including how it creates conflicting legal obligations for US companies and threatens the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Key Information Extracted

- Written by Darío Maestro, Legal Director at Surveillance Technology Oversight Project
- Client-side scanning: analyzes content on user devices BEFORE encryption engages
- "The cryptography may remain intact; the privacy guarantee would be functionally zapped"
- US companies face impossible choice between EU compliance and US consumer protection

- Council position makes voluntary scanning effectively mandatory through risk-mitigation duties
- Age verification requirements would force encrypted platforms to collect user data law enforcement could compel
- Regulation = immediate binding effect across all 27 member states (no national implementation needed)
- EU Data Privacy Framework could face challenge if EU itself implements mass surveillance
