# Brussels Signal — European Parliament Approves Mini-Chat Control

**URL:** https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/07/european-parliament-approves-mini-chat-control/

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

News report on the July 9, 2026 vote to extend Chat Control 1.0 through 2028.

## Key Information Extracted

- Motion to reject failed to achieve absolute majority needed
- Extension runs until April 3, 2028
- Allows (does not require) scanning for already-identified CSAM
- US tech companies affected: Instagram, Discord, Snapchat, Xbox, Gmail, iCloud
- Does NOT mandate scanning, does NOT require breaking E2EE, does NOT give governments direct access
- True E2EE services not subject to routine scanning under these interim rules
- Permanent Chat Control (CSAR) still being negotiated separately
- Digital rights groups criticised the process as undermining democratic scrutiny
- Patrick Breyer: "passed against the will of majority of voting MEPs — a farce that damages democracy"
