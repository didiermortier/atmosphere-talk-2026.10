# EU Social Media Age Restrictions — Under-16 Ban Proposals

**URL:** https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-to-propose-new-legislation-on-minors-protection-on-thursday/

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

The EU's proposed KIDS Act to restrict minors' access to social media and video-sharing platforms before age 15. Includes age verification requirements and tiered access.

## Key Information Extracted

- **EU KIDS Act**: Commission proposal to restrict social media access
- **Under 13**: Parents can open fully controlled accounts
- **13-15**: Introductory accounts with limits on contact with strangers and screen time
- **After 15**: Teenagers can set up own accounts
- Requires age verification technology
- Applied to social media, video platforms, online games, AI companions
- Excludes educational tools
- Centralised enforcement similar to DSA and AI Act

### National-level actions (from Interface.eu)
- **France**: First EU country to ban social media for under-15s (July 2026)
- **Poland**: Draft law banning under-15s, requiring age verification
- **Italy**: Bill proposing strict ban under 15, parental consent 15-16
- **Germany**: CDU/SPD propose ban under 14, restricted access 14-16
- **Spain**: Parliament approved raising data consent age to 16
- **Portugal**: Bill forbids access under 13, parental consent 13-16
- **Australia**: First country worldwide to ban under-16s (Dec 2025)
- **23 of 27 EU Member States** contemplating national legislation
- **Jutland Declaration**: 25 EU states + Norway/Iceland signed commitment (Oct 2025)
- European Commission released prototype of age verification app (April 2026)
