# WIRED — The Big Interview: Jay Graber

**URL:** https://www.wired.com/story/big-interview-jay-graber-bluesky/

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

An in-depth interview with Jay Graber about Bluesky, the AT Protocol, and the vision for the Atmosphere.

## Key Information Extracted

- Bluesky had **34.6 million users** at the time of the interview (later grew to 40M+)
- Graber sees the Atmosphere as "nothing less than the democratized future of the social internet"
- The AT Protocol: "a rule book that servers use to communicate" — open source, sovereign digital spaces
- Apps with different moderation or ad models can work together — or not. Up to them.

### Background Story
- Started as a skunkworks within Twitter in 2019
- Graber was a contractor, wanted independence from "old Twitter's slow movement"
- Jack Dorsey was the biggest champion
- When Elon Musk announced the Twitter acquisition, "no new projects were going to get shipped"
- That's when Bluesky decided to spin off and build its own app

### Key Features Discussed
- **Communities**: custom feeds that function like subreddits, with their own rules and moderation
- **Verification**: tools to authenticate identity (rolled out in stages)
- **Feeds**: anyone can build an algorithm, third-party is first party

### Size & Team
- ~25 employees at interview time
- Growing at a "sustainable pace"

### Notable Quote
- "What can you do with this?" — Graber's approach to emerging technology
- Her background in Zcash: "decentralized technology combined with privacy technology"
