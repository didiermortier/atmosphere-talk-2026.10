# Verge Decoder — Bluesky CEO Toni Schneider Interview

**URL:** https://www.theverge.com/podcast/974387/bluesky-toni-schneider-interview-ai-atproto-atmosphere

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

Comprehensive interview with Toni Schneider, Bluesky's new permanent CEO (formerly CEO of Automattic/WordPress). Covers protocol priorities, Atmosphere ecosystem, monetisation, and future vision.

## Key Information Extracted

- **Private data is the #1 protocol priority**: "the biggest thing we're working on with AT Proto is private data"
- "Once we enable private data, it's like 10 or 100X the number of use cases we can serve"
- **Reddit-style communities**: private, membership-based spaces owned by users, not by Bluesky
- **Bluesky is two things**: the app AND the protocol. Only one of over 1,000 apps
- **1,000 daily active apps** on the network (doubled since start of 2026)
- **SDK downloads tripled**, heading to 1M/month
- **10-year goal: a billion people on AT Proto**
- **Monetisation**: affiliate-style, helping creators make money, not ads
- **Atmosphere ≠ Bluesky**: goal is to make users understand their account is on the network, not in the app
- **Standard Site format**: WordPress, Leaflet, Pckt, Offprint all using same format — blog posts appear in Bluesky automatically
- **Bluesky app = "lobby to the Atmosphere"** — an on-ramp to the wider ecosystem
- **Moderation**: distributed — Blacksky runs its own moderation, others can too
- **Decentralisation**: handing protocol spec to IETF for standardisation
- **Identity directory**: the only thing no company should control
- **AI tools lowering barrier**: "you can point to AI coding tools and say 'build me a social app'"
