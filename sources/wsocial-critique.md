# W Social — TruthSocial with a European Accent

**URL:** https://wecanjustdothings.leaflet.pub/3mokohkfb4224

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

Critical analysis of W Social, a European social network launched at Davos 2026. Built on the AT Protocol but deleted its public source code. Positioned as a "trustworthy European alternative" but criticised as "TruthSocial for Liberals."

## Key Information Extracted

- Launched at Davos (January 2026), backed by Swedish investors and former ministers
- Built on AT Protocol but initially obscured this fact
- **Deleted their public GitHub repository** without explanation
- Aral Balkan: "The standard practice is to deprecate the old repo... Not delete it"
- **EU institutions moved their accounts to W Social**: European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, European Central Bank, Christine Lagarde
- Official EU data moved to a private, for-profit, Swedish company
- This happened days after Commission launched Tech Sovereignty package
- Contrast with EuroSky: non-profit, transparent, open development
- Article author discloses working as design consultant for EuroSky
- Key line: "European isn't a synonym for ethical"
