# popfeed.social — Social Media Reviews Platform

**URL:** https://popfeed.social

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

PopFeed is a social platform for logging and reviewing movies, TV shows, books, games, and music. Built on the AT Protocol.

## Key Information Extracted

- Log movies, TV, books, games, and music
- Share reviews and discover what friends are into
- Connects to TMDB for movie/TV metadata
- Reviews include ratings (star system), watched dates, platforms
- Lists/collections: curated lists like "Books read in 2026" or "Completed Games"
- Challenges feature for community engagement
- Activity feed shows real-time updates from friends
- iOS and Android apps available
- Reviews cross-post to Bluesky
- User handles are AT Protocol handles (@user.com)
- Data portable — reviews and lists are yours to keep
