# atmosphereaccount.com — Atmosphere Account

**URL:** https://atmosphereaccount.com/

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

A promotional site explaining the Atmosphere ecosystem — the network of AT Protocol apps. Explains the concept of a unified account across all apps.

## Key Information Extracted

- "The last social account you'll ever need — one account for all your apps, yours to keep wherever you go"
- The Atmosphere is an open network of apps sharing a common foundation
- Bluesky is the first Atmosphere app, but there are many more
- **One account unlocks every app** — no more creating new logins
- Not owned or controlled by any single company
- "It's an open social internet, not another 'Sign in with Google'"

### Featured Apps (on the site)
Bluesky, Spark, Margin, mu, Blacksky, Stream Place, PopFeed, Leaflet, Germ, Anisota, Tangled, Semble, and many more

### Key Features
- **Universal identity**: one @handle everywhere
- **You own your account**: not trapped in any one app
- **Anyone can build**: tap into existing network from day one
- **Custom domain handles**: use your own domain as your @handle

### Account Hosting Options
1. **Apps** (Bluesky, etc.) — create account through app
2. **Independent hosts** (EuroSky, etc.) — privacy-focused, community-run
3. **Self-host** — full control on your own infrastructure

### Moderation & Algorithms
- Subscribe to community-built moderation labels
- Feeds are open — anyone can build one, switch like playlists
- Portability — move between apps and hosts while keeping connections
