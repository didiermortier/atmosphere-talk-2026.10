# Fight Chat Control — Chat Control 1.0 vs 2.0 Overview

**URL:** https://fightchatcontrol.eu/chat-control-overview.html

**Date accessed:** 2026-09-16

## What It Covers

Comprehensive overview of both Chat Control 1.0 (temporary derogation) and Chat Control 2.0 (permanent CSA Regulation). Detailed timeline, current status, and key differences.

## Key Information Extracted

### Chat Control 1.0
- Temporary derogation from ePrivacy Directive, originally adopted 2021
- Allows (not requires) providers to voluntarily scan private messages for CSAM
- Expired April 4, 2026 after Parliament rejected extension (311-228)
- **July 9, 2026**: Revived via fast-track procedure when only 314 MEPs voted to reject (needed 361)
- Now in force until 2028
- End-to-end encrypted services exempted
- Companies like Google, Meta, Microsoft, Snap said they'd scan regardless

### Chat Control 2.0 (CSA Regulation)
- Proposed May 2022, being debated since
- Would make detection and reporting a legal requirement
- **Parliament position (2023)**: No scanning of E2EE, judicial warrants required, targeted at specific suspects
- **Council position (Nov 2025)**: Dropped mandatory detection orders, shifted to "voluntary" suspicionless detection + risk mitigation duties
- **Council's own lawyers**: "Voluntary" scanning still constitutes generalised scanning incompatible with EU Charter Article 7
- 5 trilogue rounds failed (latest June 29, 2026)
- Core disagreement: suspicionless scanning and encryption remain unresolved
- Negotiations continue under Irish presidency

### Key Statistics (from Patrick Breyer)
- 99% of Meta's reports are previously known material
- 48% of alerts not criminally relevant
- 40% of investigations target minors themselves
- Mass scanning = 36% of abuse reports in 2024
- Most reports come from public posts and cloud storage
- Commission admits no evidence scanning increased convictions or rescued more children
