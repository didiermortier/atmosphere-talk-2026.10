# Jay Graber — Wikipedia

## What It Covers

Biography of Jay Graber, the first CEO of Bluesky and creator of the AT Protocol vision.

## Key Information Extracted

- Born **1991** in Tulsa, Oklahoma
- Full name: **Lantian Graber** — "Lantian" means "blue sky" in Mandarin Chinese
- Mother is Chinese-American (acupuncturist), father is Swiss-American (math teacher)
- Mother named her Lantian as a wish for "boundless freedom"
- The similarity between her name and Bluesky is **purely coincidental** — Jack Dorsey chose the name
- Graduated University of Pennsylvania (BA in Science, Technology, and Society)

### Career Timeline
- **2014-2015**: Software engineer at SkuChain
- **2015**: Worked in a factory soldering bitcoin mining equipment
- **2016**: Junior developer for Zcash cryptocurrency
- **2019**: Founded Happening Inc. (event planning)
- **2021 (Aug)**: Became first CEO of Bluesky
- **2023**: Bluesky launches as invite-only
- **2025**: SXSW — wears "mundus sine caesaribus" shirt (jab at Zuckerberg's "aut Zuck aut nihil")
- **2026 (Mar)**: Steps down as CEO, becomes Chief Innovation Officer
- Toni Schneider serves as interim CEO

### Key Quotes / Notes
- "A world without caesars" — her shirt's Latin motto
- Bluesky described as "billionaire-proof"
- 40M+ users reached during her tenure
