# The Atmosphere: Building the Open Social Web

**Audience:** Developers  
**Languages:** English and Spanish, switchable in the deck  
**Format:** One self-contained HTML file, works offline

---

## Chapter Outline

### 1 — Personal Story: From Meta to Bluesky
Who I am, my background, quitting Meta products, switching to Signal, discovering Bluesky.

### 2 — The Story So Far: Timeline + What is AT Protocol
From 2019 Twitter skunkworks to 46M+ accounts today. The protocol explained in between the timeline milestones: DIDs, PDS, relays, Lexicons. The Open Social philosophy. Why it's different from ActivityPub. The Atmosphere ecosystem.

### 3 — The Ecosystem Today: The Good, the Neutral, and the Bad
What's working (apps, feeds, communities). What's struggling (scale, moderation, adoption). Real projects bridging to AT Protocol.

### 4 — Social Media and Young People: Why the Atmosphere Is Better
Social media restrictions for under-16s (EU KIDS Act, France, Australia). Why the Atmosphere is a better alternative — no forced algorithms, choice, portability.

### 5 — The Future
Roadmap, what's coming. Opportunities for developers. Call to action.
